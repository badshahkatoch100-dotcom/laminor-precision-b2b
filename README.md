# Laminor Precision Pastry

Create a high end B2B bakery website for a premium artisanal pastry brand named LAMINOR.

This is a luxury, chef driven pastry house supplying restaurants, cafés, hotels, and premium events.
The goal is to build trust fast, signal precision and reliability, and convert professional buyers.

BRAND POSITIONING

LAMINOR is an upscale artisanal bakery specializing in gourmet cakes, cheesecakes, and refined desserts.
The brand stands for precision, consistency, and premium quality.

Target audience.
Restaurant owners.
Executive chefs.
Café founders.
Hotel F and B managers.
Corporate catering buyers.

Price positioning.
Premium. Luxury. Never discount driven.

DESIGN DIRECTION

Overall style must feel French patisserie meets modern minimalism.

Tone.
Elegant. Calm. Confident.
Never playful. Never cute. Never rustic.

Layout principles.
Strong typography.
Large negative space.
Grid based structure.
Editorial spacing.

Avoid clutter.
Avoid stock looking layouts.

COLOR AND TYPOGRAPHY

Primary colors.
Deep burgundy.
Warm cream.
Champagne gold accents only.

Typography.
Primary. Elegant serif for headings.
Secondary. Clean sans serif for body text.

Text must remain readable at all sizes.

HOMEPAGE STRUCTURE
Hero Section

Full width hero.
Dark background or clean cream background.
Place uploaded Laminor logo prominently.

Headline example.
“Precision pastry for professional kitchens.”

Subheadline.
Artisanal cakes and cheesecakes crafted for restaurants, cafés, and premium events.

Primary CTA button.
“Request Product List”

Secondary CTA.
“Book a Tasting”

Trust Section

Short credibility focused block.

Include points like.
Professional pastry chefs.
Consistent production.
Reliable delivery.
Premium ingredients.

No long paragraphs.

Product Overview Section

Show products in a clean grid.
Minimal photography.
Short names only.

Include categories.
Cakes.
Cheesecakes.
Pies.

Do not show prices.

CTA below.
“View Full Product Range”

B2B Focus Section

Explain how Laminor works with businesses.

Key points.
Weekly supply.
Custom orders.
Volume consistency.
Clear ordering process.

CTA.
“Become a Partner”

Quality and Process Section

Minimal text.
Focus on discipline and craft.

Key ideas.
Controlled recipes.
Precise baking.
Quality checks.

Avoid romantic storytelling.

Contact and Lead Capture

Simple professional form.

Fields.
Name.
Business name.
Email.
Phone.
Type of business.

CTA button.
“Request Collaboration”

INNER PAGES REQUIRED

About Laminor
Short. Professional. Chef focused.

Products
Clean catalog. Categories only.

B2B Partnerships
Process. Expectations. Reliability.

Contact
Direct. No clutter.

IMAGE STYLE

Use dark, moody, premium food photography.
Focus on texture and structure.
No busy backgrounds.
No lifestyle shots.

UX REQUIREMENTS

Fast loading.
Mobile first.
Clear CTAs.
Easy navigation.

No pop ups.
No animations that distract.

FINAL OUTPUT GOAL

The website must feel like a supplier trusted by Michelin level kitchens, even if the business is new.

A visitor should think.
“These people are serious.”
“They understand precision.”
“I can trust them with my menu.”
